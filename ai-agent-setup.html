Common Questions

Do I own the AI agents?

Yes. Your trained prompts, your data, and your configurations are yours. If we ever part ways, you keep everything — we hand over full documentation.

How long until it's live?

Essential builds go live in about 3 weeks. Growth in 4–5 weeks. Elite is phased over 6–8 weeks. The clock starts the day your intake questionnaire comes back.

What's the contract term?

Builds start with a 50% deposit; the balance is due at go-live after your approval walkthrough. Retainers: the first 90 days are committed — agents need a real tuning cycle to prove out — then month-to-month. No multi-year lock-ins.

What if the agent gives a wrong answer?

Complex or sensitive conversations escalate to your team automatically, with full context attached. We review interactions weekly during the first 60 days and tune continuously — that's what the retainer is for.
